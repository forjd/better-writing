In today's fast-paced world, remote work has become a cornerstone of modern business. At Hollis & Grant, we made the switch in March 2024, and it's not just a policy change — it's a transformation. Our 42-person team now works from four countries, and we meet in person once a quarter in Bristol. Moreover, we've seamlessly integrated async stand-ups, fostering a culture of trust and flexibility. Ultimately, the future of work is here, and we're proud to be leading the way.
